Thelora is a realm shaped by the Loom—a divine weave of memory, prophecy, and time. Its lands shimmer with ancient echoes, and every soul is a thread in the tapestry of fate. Seasons bend, stars whisper, and the past is never truly gone.

The Loom

Neither god nor machine, the Loom pulses beneath reality. It binds seasons to stone, dreams to echoes, and destinies to stars. Those who walk its threads may glimpse what was, or what may yet be.

The Weaveborn

The weave is in danger. Powerful forces are at work, the very fate of the world hangs in the blance. Although the party's actions may seem small, it may be the deciding factor in which way the scales will tip.

Chronicle of Thelora

    Our story begins in the quiet town of Dunmere. Dunmere rests at the wooded edge of the Shattered Plains, a quiet village of hearths and fields where life follows the rhythm of the seasons. Its people are bound by tradition and resilience, tending crops, hunting the forest, and gathering in the inn’s warm glow — yet the land beyond whispers of paths that lead far from home.

    Edrin Hollowbrook - the elder, keeper of lore and village council head.

    Godric Hale - the reeve, stern but fair, settles disputes and keeps order.

    Torren Greyforge — the blacksmith, whose forge is the heartbeat of the town.

    Maris Thornfield — the innkeeper, source of gossip and hospitality.

    Seren Vale — the warden of the woods, hunter and protector of the forest paths.

    Alira Dawnwell — local wisdom, trusted for healing and counsil.
