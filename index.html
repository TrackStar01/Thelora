Thelora is a realm shaped by the Loom—a divine weave of memory, prophecy, and time. Its lands shimmer with ancient echoes, and every soul is a thread in the tapestry of fate. Seasons bend, stars whisper, and the past is never truly gone.

The Loom

Neither god nor machine, the Loom pulses beneath reality. It binds seasons to stone, dreams to echoes, and destinies to stars. Those who walk its threads may glimpse what was, or what may yet be.

The Weaveborn

The weave is in danger. Powerful forces are at work, the very fate of the world hangs in the blance. Although the party's actions may seem small, it may be the deciding factor in which way the scales will tip.

Chronicle of Thelora

    Our story begins in the quiet town of Dunmere.

    Dunmere rests at the wooded edge of the Shattered Plains, a quiet village of hearths and fields where life follows the rhythm of the seasons. This year, however, the rhythm has faltered — the winter has lingered longer than any remember, and food stores grow thin. If spring does not come soon, it'll be a rough harvest and hard times will soon fall upon the people. Yet the folk of Dunmere are not ones to sit idle; they square their shoulders and work with what they are given, making do with resilience and resolve. Even now, preparations are underway for the yearly winter festival, a celebration of hearth and community that carries them through the cold with laughter, song, and tradition. Guided by figures like Edrin Hollowbrook, the elder, and Godric Hale, the reeve, the villagers cling to their old ways, even as the forest beyond whispers of paths that lead far from home.

    🎉 The Winter Festival

    Feast in the Hall: Families bring what little food remains — roasted game, root vegetables, and spiced cider — shared communally to remind all that no one faces hardship alone.

    Lantern Lighting: Children craft lanterns from wood and hide, lit with flickering candles to “call spring back to the land.”

    Storytelling Circle: Elders recount tales of past winters survived, weaving myth and memory into lessons for the young.

    Games & Contests: Simple competitions — log‑splitting, archery, or footraces in the snow — keep spirits high.

    Music & Dance: Fiddles, drums, and voices rise in song, echoing through the frosted streets until dawn.

    Edrin Hollowbrook - the elder, keeper of lore and village council head.

    Godric Hale - the reeve, stern but fair, settles disputes and keeps order.

    Torren Greyforge — the blacksmith, whose forge is the heartbeat of the town.

    Maris Thornfield — the innkeeper, source of gossip and hospitality.

    Seren Vale — the warden of the woods, hunter and protector of the forest paths.

    Alira Dawnwell — local wisdom, trusted for healing and counsil.
